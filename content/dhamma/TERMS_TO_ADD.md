### Proposed → VIII.a (sub-cluster off `sammā-diṭṭhi`)

The three views mundane sammā-diṭṭhi (MN 117) is defined against — the contrast-class for affirmative kamma-cosmology. Canonical loci: DN 2 Sāmaññaphala-sutta (Six Heretical Teachers), MN 60 Apaṇṇaka-sutta, MN 76 Sandaka-sutta.

- **natthika-diṭṭhi** (Skt: nāstika-dṛṣṭi) — the "[there]-is-not" view; standardly rendered "nihilism." Ajita Kesakambala's position. Denies a specific bundle: kamma-fruit, other world, mother/father (as moral objects), spontaneously-arising beings, awakened ones who have realized [the truth] for themselves. _def-flag: "nihilism" is the standard English rendering but is misleading at the EBT level — Ajita's underlying ontology was materialist (four-elements; not a denial of existence as such). `natthika-diṭṭhi` names a specific bundle of denials on top of that ontology, not a global "nothing exists" claim. Distinct from the abstract `natthitā` (existence/non-existence pair, X.b → SN 12.15 Kaccānagotta), which IS the bare "nothing exists" pole and which Nāgārjuna inherits at MMK 15.7. The two threads share the `na atthi` root but pick out different doctrinal targets — Madhyamaka's `nāstika`-charge engages the abstract pair, not Ajita's bundle._
- **akiriya-diṭṭhi** (Skt: akriyā-dṛṣṭi) — the "no-action" view; Pūraṇa Kassapa's position. Denies that actions have moral weight (no good or evil done by acting).
- **ahetuka-diṭṭhi** (Skt: ahetuka-dṛṣṭi) — the "no-cause" view; Makkhali Gosāla's position (Ājīvika determinism). Denies causal conditioning of defilement and purification — beings are defiled / purified without cause.

### Proposed → X.b (limit-points of doctrine; Kaccānagotta middle-way framing)

The view-pairs the middle way is defined against — feed forward into Madhyamaka's reception of SN 12.15 at XII.b.

- **sassata-diṭṭhi** (Skt: śāśvata-dṛṣṭi) — eternalism; the self exists and persists eternally. DN 1 Brahmajāla catalogues four varieties.
- **uccheda-diṭṭhi** (Skt: uccheda-dṛṣṭi) — annihilationism; lit. "cutting-off view" (ud-√chid). Presupposes a real self now, cut off at death. DN 1 catalogues seven varieties (gross material self, divine sense-sphere, form-sphere, formless-sphere selves).
- **atthitā** (Skt: astitā) / **natthitā** (Skt: nāstitā) — existence / non-existence; the limit-point pair from SN 12.15 Kaccānagotta-sutta that the middle way ("by-and-large this world depends on a duality, on the notion of existence and the notion of non-existence...") explicitly transcends. Doctrinally load-bearing because Nāgārjuna cites this sutta directly at MMK 15.7 — the EBT framing is the source for the Madhyamaka critique of svabhāva.
